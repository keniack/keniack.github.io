About

A technical career spanning industry, research, and venture building

My work sits at the intersection of performance engineering, distributed intelligence, and mission-driven infrastructure. I spent a decade building backend systems for telecom billing, payments, and streaming at Hutchison Drei Austria while completing advanced studies at TU Wien.

Today I combine academic research with product and technical leadership. My research focuses on serverless computing, the edge-cloud continuum, IoT, edge intelligence, and orbital edge computing, including early space computing efforts at TU Wien's Distributed Systems Group.
